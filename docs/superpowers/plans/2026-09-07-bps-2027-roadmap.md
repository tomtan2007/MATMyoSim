## Global Constraints

- Do not interpret a free fitted `k_2/k_1` ratio biologically.
- Keep Control mavacamten timing and fitted parameters provisional until stimulus synchronization is clarified.
- Distinguish practical multistart identifiability from structural identifiability.
- Do not claim population-level effects from representative single-cell traces.
- Compare AIC only among models fitted to the same genotype, target, scoring window, and alignment policy.
- Preserve all raw data, historical outputs, and run-capsule provenance.
- Use no more than two simultaneous MATLAB instances; production fitting remains serial by default.
- No slide deck is required for the current analysis phase; the conference deliverable is a poster.

## Default Conference Story

The default abstract and poster narrative is:

1. **Model problem:** Existing lower-state models may not capture the myosin-state mechanisms needed to reproduce twitch force and timing.
2. **Model contribution:** Build and mathematically verify the 6-state SRX/DRX/crossbridge model.
3. **Model comparison:** Compare 2-, 3-, 4-, and 6-state descriptions using fit quality and AIC on matched data and assumptions.
4. **Parameter insight:** Use parameter sweeps and identifiability-aware fitting to identify which kinetic parameters control force amplitude, SRX occupancy, contraction timing, and relaxation.
5. **Experimental application:** Use mavacamten as a perturbation test asking whether those predicted parameter sensitivities explain an observed reduction in force and altered waveform shape.
6. **Multiscale relevance:** Provide defensible force-generating parameter changes for later use in Julia's cell/heart-scale model.

Mavacamten is therefore supporting evidence by default, not the headline. It becomes central only after explicit PI approval and only if its timing provenance, fit improvement, and parameter identifiability are strong.
