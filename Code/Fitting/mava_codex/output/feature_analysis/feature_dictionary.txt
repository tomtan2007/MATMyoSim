Thresholds are relative to the baseline-corrected robust peak.
relaxation_50_time: peak-centroid to 50% remaining force.
relaxation_90_time: peak-centroid to 10% remaining force.
duration_above_X: rising-to-falling width at X% amplitude.
plateau_duration: contiguous time at or above 95% amplitude.
auc_normalized: full available positive AUC divided by peak amplitude.
auc_to_relax50_normalized: comparable shape AUC ending at 50% relaxation.
auc_to_relax90_normalized: shape AUC ending at 90% relaxation when observed.
max/min_force_rate: force derivatives, not shortening velocity.
decay_X_from_plateau_end: decay timing with broad peak duration removed.
